# Purnakosh

**Your Money. Your Assets. Your Complete Picture.**

Purnakosh is a personal finance app for households in Nepal: NEPSE shares, money in and out, income tax, and vehicle
tax, kept on your device and in your own Google Drive.

This repository holds the Android app's releases only.

## Download

Get the latest APK from **[Releases](https://github.com/shrestha-tech/koshvero-releases/releases/latest)**: download
`purnakosh-vX.Y.Z.apk` (older ones: `koshvero-…`), open it on your phone and tap **Install** (or **Update**). Android asks once to allow installs
from your browser or file manager; on Samsung phones, turn off **Auto Blocker** first (Settings → Security and
privacy → Auto Blocker). Once installed, most updates arrive inside the app.

Prefer the browser? Use the [web app](https://purnakosh.nepali-tech.workers.dev/).
